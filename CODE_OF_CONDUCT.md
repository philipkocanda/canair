# Code of Conduct

canair is a hobbyist project for people curious about how their cars work. Keep
it that way:

- **Be kind and constructive.** Assume good faith. Critique ideas, not people.
- **Help newcomers.** Everyone was new to CAN reverse-engineering once. A patient
  answer is worth more than a clever one.
- **Stay on topic and honest.** Share evidence for decoded signals; mark guesses
  as guesses. Don't misrepresent what you've verified.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks** — in issues, pull
  requests, or anywhere else in the project.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, commit messages, and
any other project communication.

## Enforcement

If someone's behavior is a problem, contact the maintainer at
**philip@kocanda.nl**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit,
hide, or remove contributions and comments that violate this code, and may block
repeat or serious offenders from the project.
